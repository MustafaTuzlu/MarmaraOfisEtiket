Your Partner for Barcode and Label Systems

Marmara Ofis Etiket supplies barcode printers, barcode labels, ribbons, hand terminals and barcode readers from one place in Halkalı, Istanbul. We produce thermal, coated, vellum and opaque labels in the size you need and help you choose the right ribbon and label for your printer.

Product Groups

  • Labels: thermal, coated, vellum and opaque barcode labels
  • Ribbons: wax, wax-resin and resin ribbons
  • Barcode Printers: Argox, TSC, Toshiba and Zebra printers
  • Hand Terminals: terminals for stock counting and inventory
  • Barcode Readers: corded, cordless and 2D readers

Why Marmara Ofis Etiket?

  • Custom-size label production
  • Guidance on printer, ribbon and label compatibility
  • Independent logistics services for fast, organised delivery
  • Local support in Halkalı, Istanbul

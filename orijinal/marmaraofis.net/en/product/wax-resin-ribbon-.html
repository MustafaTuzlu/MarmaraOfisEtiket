Wax Resin Ribbon

Wax Resin Ribbon 

high-performance ribbons that can print on paper and synthetic labels. 

Wax / resin ribbons give more permanent pressure than wax ribbons, the scratch and abrasion resistance is higher than wax ribbons and less than resin ribbons. Resistance to environmental conditions is high. Wax / resin ribbons can be used in barcode printers (such as industrial printers from Toshiba Tec and Avery Dennison brands) that use both flat head and near edge (45 degree slant) heads.
                                                            

Wax / resin ribbons can be printed on plastic-derived PP, PE, and PVC labels, including wax ribbons.
